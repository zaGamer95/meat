    Grades

    USDA grading is about marbling — the flecks of intramuscular fat — and the age of the animal. It is voluntary, so ungraded beef is not necessarily worse, just unassessed.

    GradeMarblingWorth it for
    PrimeAbundantDry-heat cuts where fat is the point — ribeye, strip.
    ChoiceModerateThe sensible default for most cooking.
    SelectSlightLean and cheap; marinate, or braise it.
    Grade does not predict flavour on its own. A Choice chuck roast braised properly beats a Prime tenderloin cooked carelessly. Grade matters most when the fat has no time to render — that is, for fast, dry heat.
